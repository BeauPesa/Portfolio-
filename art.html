Off the clock

Creative Endeavors.

I hold a classical illustration degree, and drawing was my first design discipline, long before software was. A watercolor I painted of São Paulo's hydrology ended up on the cover of my graduate thesis and in front of the city's mayor.

Most of what I make outside of work is analog on purpose — sketching, restoring an old sailboat back to working condition, and getting lost in horticulture. It's the same instinct as the UX work, just without a screen: pay attention, find what's not working, fix it by hand.
