How I work

A look backstage.

I'm comfortable with ambiguity and biased toward making. Most of my process is the same regardless of medium: understand the constraint, find the smallest version of the real problem, then build something someone can react to.

That's shown up as constructed wetlands, network diagnostic tools, self-checkout flows, and now a warehouse app — different fields, same instinct for finding the friction and removing it.

I sketch fast, prototype faster, and would rather ship something rough and learn from it than polish a guess.
